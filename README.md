# Craving Roulette · وش نأكل؟

Can't decide what to eat? Swipe through nearby food like Tinder. Craving Roulette learns what you like and settles on a pick in about 30 seconds.

## How it works

- **Just me:** swipe right on places you'd eat, left to skip, and get tonight's winner.
- **Settle a debate:** pick how many people are deciding, then pass the phone around. Each player swipes privately, and the app finds the place everyone can agree on.
- **Find food near me** uses your location to suggest nearby restaurants. Or skip location and let it pick for you.
